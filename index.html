The Challenge

Problem Statement

The Indian Legal Crisis

▸

5.3 crore pending cases across Indian courts with no end in sight

▸

Lawyers manually review 100+ page documents to extract key facts — taking days

▸

Finding relevant Supreme/High Court precedents takes hours to days of manual database mining

▸

Drafting standard legal documents (NDAs, Bail Petitions, Legal Notices) is repetitive and expensive

▸

No way to stress-test legal arguments or simulate trial strategy before a real hearing

Result: Slow justice delivery and prohibitively high legal costs for citizens & small businesses

Existing Solutions — Why They Fail

• Tools like LawGeex and Kira are prohibitively expensive and require custom ML training

• Western-centric platforms don't understand Indian law, IPC sections, or local court language

• No unified platform — professionals bounce between editors, search engines, and transcription services

• Zero voice AI or courtroom simulation capability exists in the Indian legal tech market

Our Innovation

A unified, AI-automated legal hub specifically calibrated for the Indian legal system — combining RAG, LLMs, Court Simulation, Voice AI, Document Drafting, and Precedent Search in a single React interface.

Powered by local Ollama models + cloud Gemini & NVIDIA NIM with zero vendor lock-in.

Platform Capabilities

8 AI-Powered Modules

Each module is a standalone tool, and every tool is deeply integrated — all accessible from a single sidebar-navigated interface.

🏠

Dashboard

A centralized overview and navigation hub that surfaces quick-access to all 7 tools, platform health status, and usage summary — the command center for the entire legal workflow.

🔍

Document Q&A (RAG)

Upload any legal PDF or TXT. The system embeds the document into ChromaDB via all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings, retrieves the most relevant passages via vector similarity, and streams a grounded AI answer. Supports NVIDIA NIM pipeline with live SSE step visualization.

ChromaDB LangChain RAG NVIDIA NIM SSE Streaming
🤖

Legal Chatbot

A conversational assistant for general Indian legal queries. Powered by local Ollama (llama3.2) for privacy-first responses or NVIDIA NIM (DeepSeek-R1 / QwQ-32B) for higher reasoning. Maintains conversation history and streams responses token-by-token.

Ollama llama3.2 DeepSeek-R1 Streaming
⚖️

Courtroom Simulator

Provide case facts and legal category. The platform instantiates Prosecution and Defense LangChain agents backed by ChromaDB retrieval. Conducts 5 debate rounds with each side arguing from Indian law precedents. An AI Judge agent delivers a structured ruling, and the full transcript is exported as a formatted PDF via ReportLab.

LangChain Agents ChromaDB ReportLab PDF
📝

Legal Drafter

Auto-generate Indian court-ready legal documents from user-supplied parameters. Supports NDA, Legal Notice, Bail Petition, Rental/Lease Agreement, Affidavit, Power of Attorney, Consumer Complaint, Employment Agreement, and more. Output available as PDF (ReportLab) or DOCX with proper Indian court formatting.

9+ Doc Types PDF & DOCX Indian Formatting
🔎

Judgment Finder

Search 5 real, relevant Indian Supreme/High Court precedents. Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash, results stream card-by-card via SSE so the UI animates as each judgment arrives. Each card displays Title, Citation, Court, Year, Judges, Key Sections, Facts, Decision, Legal Ratio, and Relevance (HIGH/MEDIUM).

Gemini 2.5 Flash SSE Streaming SC + HC
📄

Document Summarizer

AI-powered summarization for legal documents. Accepts pasted text or direct file upload and returns a concise, structured summary — ideal for quickly understanding lengthy judgments, agreements, or FIR documents before diving deeper.

Paste or Upload Structured Output
📞

Voice Call Assistant

Initiate real-time outbound AI legal consultation calls via Vapi. The assistant conducts the legal consultation, handles the full transcript via webhook, and automatically generates a structured PDF report from the call. Reports are stored and downloadable from the platform. Democratizes legal access for citizens.

Vapi Voice AI Webhook Auto PDF Report

Deep Dive

How Key Modules Work

🔍

Document Q&A — RAG Pipeline

1

Upload PDF / TXT

Document is chunked and ingested into the system

2

Embed with all-MiniLM-L6-v2

Each chunk converted to a 384-dim dense vector

3

Store in ChromaDB

Persistent local vector store, keyed by session_id

4

Query → Vector Similarity Retrieval

Top-k relevant chunks fetched from ChromaDB

5

LLM Generates Grounded Answer

Ollama / NVIDIA NIM streams the final response

⚖️

Courtroom Simulator — Flow

1

Input Case Facts + Legal Category

User provides case name, facts, and selects category (Criminal, Civil, etc.)

2

Instantiate LangChain Agents

Prosecution Agent + Defense Agent initialized with ChromaDB retrieval

3

5 Debate Rounds

Each agent argues using Indian law precedents from the vector store

4

AI Judge Delivers Ruling

Judge agent evaluates both sides and produces a structured verdict

5

Export Transcript as PDF

Full debate + verdict packaged as a ReportLab PDF
